## Before you rely on these commercially

The Pexels License is permissive about the *photograph*, but it does not grant
rights in the **people depicted**. Pexels says plainly that content "may be
subject to additional intellectual property rights, including identifiable
people", and that you must consider whether you need a third party's consent.
There is no model release behind these images.

In practice, using them as generic illustration is ordinary and low-risk. It
becomes a different question if a photograph is used so as to imply that the
person shown endorses the product, is a customer, or is a member of a
particular cooperative. Avoid captions that make that claim.

The durable fix is to replace these with photographs of real cooperatives who
have agreed to appear — which is also what will make the page look like your
product rather than like stock. Treat these as a good starting point with a
short shelf life.
